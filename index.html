Research

Materials. Devices. Integration.

01 / 2D transistors

2D transistors & gate dielectrics

I study how metal electrodes and ultrathin hBN dielectrics influence leakage, reliability, and the electrical performance of MoS₂ transistors. My work connects materials selection with the design of low-voltage electronic devices.

hBNMoS₂Gate dielectrics

02 / Memristors & reliability

Memristors & material defects

I investigate how defects in hBN affect resistive switching, device variability, and circuit yield. By combining microscopy with electrical measurements, I connect local material structure with the behaviour of memristive devices.

Defect engineeringMemristorsCharacterization
Read the Advanced Materials paper ↗
03 / Circuits & integration

From 2D materials to microchips

My research explores the integration of hBN into working microchips. Collaborative studies extend this work to hybrid 2D–CMOS circuits, millimetre-wave memristive circuits, and reconfigurable GaN platforms with hBN switches.

About

A background in materials.
A focus on devices.

I’m a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Carnegie Mellon University, working with Professor Xu Zhang. My research connects two-dimensional materials, device physics, and nanoelectronic integration.

I earned my Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering at KAUST, where I worked with Professor Mario Lanza. My work spans hBN gate dielectrics, transistor engineering, memristor reliability, and the integration of 2D materials into microchips.
