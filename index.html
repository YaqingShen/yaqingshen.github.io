ACS Nano · 2025

Low-voltage MoS₂ transistors

Abstract — summary

A 4 nm hBN gate dielectric and platinum electrode enable MoS₂ transistors with a threshold voltage of 0.46 V and an ON/OFF current ratio above 10⁵. Low gate leakage and continued operation after 1,000 switching cycles demonstrate the potential of ultrathin hBN for nanoscale transistors.

Nature Electronics · 2024

Reliable gate dielectrics for 2D transistors

Abstract — summary

Platinum and tungsten gate electrodes suppress leakage through CVD-grown hBN, enabling its use as a dielectric in 2D transistors. Measurements across 867 devices, including structures on a CMOS microchip, connect electrode selection with improved dielectric reliability.

Advanced Materials · 2021

Defects, variability & memristor yield

Abstract — summary

Local defects can limit the performance of devices made from 2D materials. This study shows that vertical hBN memristors tolerate many of these defects because current follows localized conductive paths, offering a route to circuits with improved yield and consistency.
